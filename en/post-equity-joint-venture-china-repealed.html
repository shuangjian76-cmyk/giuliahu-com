The Chinese «Equity Joint Venture» no longer exists as a corporate form

Published on 13 October 2026 · Category: societario

Setting up a company with Chinese partners today means applying the Company Law, not joint-venture law. The deciding body has changed, and the minority shareholder's automatic protection is gone.

It is the question that comes up most often, and almost always in the same words: «I would like some information on Equity Joint Ventures, liability and management». The useful answer is that the corporate form no longer exists, and that the difference is not a matter of terminology.

What was repealed, and when

The Foreign Investment Law, in force since 1 January 2020, repealed the three historic foreign-investment statutes: Equity Joint Venture, Contractual Joint Venture and wholly foreign-owned enterprise. Foreign-invested companies have since followed the Company Law, the same one that applies to domestic companies. The five-year transition for existing companies expired on 31 December 2024 and, for a newly incorporated company, it never existed: the Company Law applies from day one.

The consequence that actually bites: the minority is no longer protected by statute

Under the old regime the supreme body was the board of directors, and on certain fundamental matters the law required unanimity: a 30% shareholder was protected regardless of how the articles were drafted. Today the supreme body is the shareholders' meeting and voting follows capital. Minority protection exists only if it has been written into the articles.

In practice: signing standard articles with a minority stake means accepting that the majority decides everything, including diluting your own holding. The matters to be reserved to a qualified majority or to unanimity must be listed one by one, before signature.

Two traps in the Company Law that concern money

  • Subscribed capital must be paid up within five years of incorporation. There is no longer any capital declared and paid «some day»: it is a debt with a date.
  • If one shareholder fails to pay up, or contributes assets at an inflated value, the other founding shareholders are jointly liable. If the local partner contributes a building or a trade mark and overstates its value, the shortfall is borne also by whoever paid in cash.
  • If the company fails to pay its debts when due, creditors may demand early payment of capital not yet contributed.

Where a distant partner is actually controlled

Clauses in the articles matter, but the firmest lever for a foreign shareholder sits outside the company: the trade mark, the format and the supply of goods licensed from Italy and revocable. Whoever receives no goods sells nothing, and that holds regardless of percentages — precisely when relations sour, which is when a clause is supposed to work.

A note on timing: in China a trade mark is acquired by filing, not by use. It should be filed before approaching partners and suppliers, otherwise anyone may register it and then sell it back.

Dividends from China: from 1 January 2026 it matters who holds the shares

The new double tax treaty takes effect in 2026. The reduced 5% rate is reserved to companies, and the foreign tax credit may not be available: holding personally or through a company is not the same thing.

«Not on the prohibited list» is true, and it is not enough

There are three gates into the Chinese market, not one. The negative list says whether a foreign investor may enter a sector; it does not say whether the activity needs a licence, and licences do not look at nationality.
