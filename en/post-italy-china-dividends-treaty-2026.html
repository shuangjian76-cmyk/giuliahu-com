Dividends from China: from 1 January 2026 it matters who holds the shares

Published on 12 October 2026 · Category: fiscale

The new double tax treaty takes effect in 2026. The reduced 5% rate is reserved to companies, and the foreign tax credit may not be available: holding personally or through a company is not the same thing.

Those who set up a company in China almost always leave until last the decision that costs the most: whether to hold the shares personally or through their own Italian company. After incorporation that choice can only be changed by transferring shares.

What has changed

The treaty in force until 2025 was the 1986 one, with a single 10% withholding on dividends. The new treaty, signed in 2019 and ratified by Law 182/2024, takes effect on 1 January 2026. Two changes matter more than the rate: the reduction to 5% is reserved to companies holding a qualified participation for a minimum period, and the new article on the method for eliminating double taxation denies the foreign tax credit where the income is subject to a substitute tax in Italy.

Why the identity of the holder weighs more than the rate

An individual cannot meet the corporate threshold: the Chinese withholding stays at 10%, and in Italy a foreign dividend from a non-qualified holding is subject to a substitute tax. That is precisely the case where the credit may be unavailable, and the two charges add up instead of offsetting.

An Italian company holding the qualified threshold for the required period obtains the reduced withholding, and the incoming dividend falls under the participation-exemption regime for dividends. The overall burden is of a different order of magnitude.

How the decision is actually made

  • Before incorporation: decide who the shareholder will be and, if it is an Italian company, check that the threshold and the holding period are satisfied from day one.
  • Check that the Chinese company will be able to distribute: statutory reserves, paid-up capital, the foreign-exchange documentation required for repatriation.
  • Compare the overall burden under both scenarios with your own accountant: the Italian side depends on the regime applicable to the shareholder and cannot be read off the treaty.
