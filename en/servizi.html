Services

What I solve

Six areas. They normally arrive together — which is precisely the point.

01

Company formation and choice of vehicle

Limited company, branch or representative office: three routes with opposite tax consequences, and the choice is made before the notary, not after. Incorporation, Chamber of Commerce registration, VAT number, assistance with opening the bank account.

  • Side-by-side comparison of the three forms with the tax burden of each
  • Typical timeline 4–8 weeks
  • Non-resident director: effects on corporate tax residence assessed in advance
02

Tax and accounting: analysis and coordination

Bookkeeping, statutory accounts, tax returns; corporate income tax at 24% plus regional production tax generally at 3.9%; application of the Italy–China double tax treaty; transfer pricing; Patent Box and R&D tax credit; preparation of the documentation and of the statement of facts for the licensed defence counsel.

  • Audits and assessments: document gathering and reconstruction of the facts; the adversarial procedure is conducted by the licensed professional
  • Tax monitoring and foreign asset reporting
  • Cross-border dividends and royalties
03

Employment, HR and industrial relations

Selection and application of the national collective agreement, contract design, labour cost calculation (social contributions around 40% of pay), organisational design, job descriptions and assessment grids, support during interviews, performance and training systems, preparation of the dispute file; representation is handled by the appointed lawyer or labour consultant.

  • Labour cost calculated before hiring, not after
  • Contracting chains and joint liability: compliance certificates
  • Disputes: file preparation and dealings with trade unions; representation is handled by the appointed professional
04

Immigration, secondment and settling in

Visas and residence permits, intra-corporate transfer route, family reunification, long-term residence and citizenship. For seconded staff: Italian digital identity, personal tax return, utilities.

  • Work permits for Chinese employees: 3–6 months, must be planned
  • Quota decree windows are short
  • Family matters handled together, not as a separate engagement
05

CE marking and machinery safety

Led by our technical director, an engineer with over 25 years in automotive and industry. Identification of applicable directives, technical file, risk analysis, EU declaration of conformity, dealings with the notified body.

  • Six stages, 6–16 weeks depending on the product
  • First technical assessment free of charge
  • New Machinery Regulation 2023/1230 fully applicable from 20 January 2027: preparation should start 12–18 months ahead
06

International contracts and language

Drafting and review of contracts with European partners, Italy–China trade compliance, GDPR for Chinese-owned companies in Europe. Document translation and negotiation interpreting across Chinese, Italian and English.

  • Bilingual contracts with clause-by-clause parallel text
  • Standard contractual clauses for data transfers to the Chinese parent
  • Interpreting at the negotiating table, not just translation of papers

Two things to plan before, not after

Capital compliance: Chinese exchange control and Italian anti-money-laundering review both require the origin and path of the funds to be traceable. The documents are prepared in advance — otherwise the bank account stalls, and this is the single most common cause of delay.

A sole director not resident in Italy may affect the determination of the company's tax residence. This is assessed before the appointment.
