How subscribing works, before you tick anything

The form sends nothing to a server: it opens your mail program with the message already written. The subscription arrives from your own address, which is both the consent and the proof that the address is yours.

Providing the data is optional: if you do not subscribe nothing changes, and you can write to us whenever you like.

There is no profiling: no tracking of opens, no click measurement, no automated decision-making.

Consent can be withdrawn at any time, as easily as it was given and without giving a reason: one line in reply to any message is enough.
