Payments

How to settle an accepted quote or an invoice already issued. The first introductory conversation stays free and carries no obligation.

The written quote comes before the payment

No engagement begins without a written quote setting out the work, the fee and the timing. You receive it by email, you accept it in writing, and only then does the work start. The first introductory conversation is free and commits you to nothing: it exists to establish whether your problem falls within our competence and, when it does not, to tell you who to turn to.

Bank transfer

This is the ordinary method. The account details and the reference to quote are stated in the accepted quote or in the invoice, and are not published on this page: bank details exposed on a website are the first thing reused in fake-invoice fraud. If you cannot find them, ask by replying to the same email that carried the document.

Card payment

Available on request. We send you a secure payment link, tied to the document being settled and valid for that document alone. Ask for it when you reply to the quote or to the invoice.

Invoicing

Every payment is accompanied by an electronic invoice transmitted through the Italian Interchange System. To issue it we need your tax code or VAT number, your address, and either your recipient code or your certified email address. If you are a private individual without a recipient code, the invoice remains available in your reserved area at the Italian Revenue Agency.

What we will never ask you

We do not ask for cash payments. We do not ask you to pay into an account held by a third party. We never notify a change of bank details by a last-minute email. If you receive a message changing the details of a payment already agreed, do not make the transfer and telephone us to check: this is the most common fraud pattern on professional-services payments, and one phone call defeats it.
